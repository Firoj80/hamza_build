1. Choose and validate

Use these examples as starting points. Confirm the problem with potential buyers before building.

Customer evidence

Ask: When did this last happen? How do you solve it now? What does it cost in time or money? What have you already tried? Would you pay to test a specific solution?

Do not treat a topic suggestion, like, view or free download as proof someone will pay. Use your own experience and check AI-generated facts.

2. Prepare an AI prompt

Copy this prompt into Gemini or another AI tool. Internet access and any provider account or subscription are separate from Launchpad Studio.

Completeness checks whether context fields are filled. It does not evaluate writing quality, factual accuracy or likelihood of sales.

3. Your 30-day post starter library

Thirty different angles using your audience, problem and resource. Edit the drafts before posting. Replace every bracketed placeholder with true information; skip any story that is not yours.

These are templates, not AI-generated fresh content or guaranteed viral posts. A copied draft stays plain text; apply any Threads formatting yourself.

4. Listing, links and scenario calculator

Hypothetical monthly scenario

This is arithmetic using your assumptions, not an earnings forecast. Set rates to zero when you have no evidence. Downloaders and purchasers may overlap; their counts must not be added as unique people.

Set fee percentage and fixed fees to your own payment provider's current pricing. The initial values are zero until you enter them. Excludes refunds, other costs and income taxes. No tips assumed. Check your provider before using the scenario.

5. Launch and measure

Work at your own pace. Creating and validating a useful paid product can take longer than seven days.

Track results

Record each post at 24 and 72 hours: views, replies, follows, tracked store visits and purchases. Compare the same reporting dates. Separate free downloads from paid sales.
